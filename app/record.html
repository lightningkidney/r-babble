Say each short sound on the same note, clearly and briefly (about a tenth of a second — "ka", not "kaaaa"), 15–20 cm from the mic in a quiet room. Keep the vowels pure: a·i·u·e·o as in Japanese (ah, ee, oo, eh, oh). Pitch can be anything comfortable; the recorder flattens each unit to the same pitch.
